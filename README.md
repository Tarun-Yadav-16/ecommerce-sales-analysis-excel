# 📊 E-Commerce Sales & Business Performance Analysis | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end **E-Commerce Data Analysis project built using Microsoft Excel**.

The dataset contains **10,000 customer orders**, which were cleaned, transformed, analyzed, and visualized to understand overall business performance.

The analysis focuses on **sales, profitability, customers, products, monthly trends, geographic performance, marketing channels, and product returns**.

An interactive Excel dashboard was created to present key KPIs and business insights in an easy-to-understand format.
 
---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Analyze monthly sales trends and MoM growth
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Evaluate product-level performance
- Compare regional business performance
- Analyze marketing channel effectiveness and ROI
- Understand product return patterns
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard
- Convert raw data into actionable business insights

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 🏆 Category Performance
**Beauty** was the leading category by both revenue and profit.

### 🌍 Regional Performance
The **North region** generated the highest overall sales and profit.

### 🌐 Marketing Revenue
**Website** generated the highest revenue among the analyzed marketing channels.

### 📧 Marketing Efficiency
**Email** delivered the highest marketing ROI.

This demonstrates an important business insight:

> The marketing channel generating the highest revenue is not necessarily the most efficient channel.

### 📦 Product Performance
Product-level analysis showed that products should not be evaluated only by sales volume. Profit and profit margin are also important when measuring performance.

### 📅 Monthly Performance
Month-over-Month analysis helped identify changes and fluctuations in sales performance throughout the year.

### 🔄 Returns
The business recorded **815 returns from 10,000 orders**, resulting in an overall return rate of **8.15%**.
